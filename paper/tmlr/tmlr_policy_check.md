# TMLR Official Policy Verification Record

**Date Checked:** September 23, 2026  
**Primary Source:** Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR) Official Portal (`https://jmlr.org/tmlr/`)

## 1. Verified Official Policies

### 1.1 Mandatory LaTeX Template & Formatting
- **Official URL:** `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html` and `https://github.com/JmlrOrg/tmlr-style-file`
- **Policy Statement:** Submissions must be PDF files generated using the official TMLR LaTeX stylefile and template (`tmlr.sty`, `tmlr.bst`).
- **Formatting Constraints:** Modifying fonts, margins, line spacing, or page geometry is strictly prohibited and grounds for desk rejection without review.
- **Length Constraint:** There is no strict maximum page limit, but the paper's length should be justified by its content. An Appendix may be included after references for proofs, derivations, or complimentary results.

### 1.2 Double-Blind Anonymization
- **Official URL:** `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html` and `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html`
- **Policy Statement:** TMLR uses a double-blind review process. Submissions must be fully anonymized.
- **Anonymization Rules:**
  - Author names, affiliations, email addresses, and acknowledgements must not appear in the submission PDF.
  - The `tmlr.sty` style file automatically hides author blocks unless the `[accepted]` or `[preprint]` options are passed.
  - Direct repository links or statements that disclose author identities must be omitted or sanitized during the review phase.
  - Self-citations are permitted when scientifically necessary, but must be phrased in neutral, third-person language (e.g., "The evaluated implementation..." rather than "Our previous implementation...").

### 1.3 LLM / Generative AI Assistance Disclosure
- **Official URL:** `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html#llm-use-policy-for-authors`
- **Policy Statement:** "LLMs may be used as general-purpose assistive tools. Whichever tools are used, authors are fully responsible for content on which they are listed as (co-) authors. This includes, but is not limited to, content generated by LLMs that could be construed as plagiarism or scientific misconduct (e.g., fabrication of facts). Low-quality contributions that appear to be largely LLM-generated will be closely examined... LLMs are not eligible for authorship."
- **Implementation Requirement:** Authors must disclose LLM assistance in a first-page footnote describing the specific assistive roles (e.g., editing, literature organization, consistency checking).

### 1.4 Broader Impact & Ethics Statement
- **Official URL:** `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/ethics.html` and `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html`
- **Policy Statement:** If the work carries potential risks or meaningful societal consequences, authors are expected to include a Broader Impact Statement.
- **Placement & Style:** Placed before the References as an unnumbered section (`\subsubsection*{Broader Impact Statement}`). Must be concise, concrete, and evidence-based, avoiding excessive speculation.

### 1.5 Supplementary Material Limits & Format
- **Official URL:** `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html`
- **Policy Statement:** Authors may submit up to **100 MB** of supplementary material (code, data, proofs). Supplementary materials must be in PDF or ZIP format and must also be strictly anonymized.

### 1.6 OpenReview Submission & Action Editor Assignment
- **Official URL:** `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html`
- **Policy Statement:** All submissions are processed through OpenReview (`https://openreview.net/group?id=TMLR`). Authors must have active OpenReview profiles with complete institutional history, conflicts of interest, and publication history. Authors must recommend appropriate Action Editors upon submission.

### 1.7 Dual Submissions & Originality
- **Official URL:** `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html`
- **Policy Statement:** TMLR accepts only original contributions. Parallel submissions to other archival peer-reviewed venues are prohibited. Overlap with non-archival workshops or preprint servers (such as arXiv) is explicitly permitted.

### 1.8 Copyright & Licensing
- **Official URL:** `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html`
- **Policy Statement:** All submissions are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). Authors retain copyright at all times. Diamond open access: $0 author processing charges (APC).
